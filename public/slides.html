OpsMentor — Embedded AI Agent

Observes every tab, acts on your behalf — chat logs it all

OpsMentor is the app's own agent — not a chatbot bolted on. It watches build state across all tabs in real time, detects EOL and incompatibilities instantly, and executes workflow actions with your permission. Chat is the activity log; voice is the action bridge.
