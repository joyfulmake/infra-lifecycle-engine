Service Level Agreement

Effective date: 21 June 2026  |  Applies to: Professional, Team, and Enterprise plans

Summary: We target 99.5% monthly uptime. Enterprise customers get 1-business-day support response. If we miss the uptime target, we credit your next invoice. Local-only (guest/starter) builds are unaffected by any service interruption — they run in your browser.

1. Uptime Commitment

OpsManifest targets 99.5% monthly uptime for the web application at opsmanifest.pages.dev. This equates to a maximum of approximately 3.6 hours of unplanned downtime per month.

Uptime is measured from our external monitoring service (UptimeRobot). The public status page is available at stats.uptimerobot.com/opsmanifest.

What counts as downtime

  • The application is unreachable (HTTP 5xx or timeout) for more than 5 consecutive minutes
  • Core workflow features (build, design, Gantt, RTM, export) are non-functional

What does not count as downtime

  • Planned maintenance with at least 48 hours advance notice
  • AI/voice features (OpsMentor, TTS) — these depend on third-party APIs and are excluded from the uptime commitment
  • Live EOL data (endoflife.date API) — third-party, excluded
  • Interruptions caused by the customer's own network, browser, or device
  • Force majeure events (natural disasters, widespread internet outages)

If monthly uptime falls below 99.5% in any calendar month, Enterprise and Team customers are entitled to a service credit applied to their next invoice:

Credits must be requested within 14 days of the incident by emailing sriram.c76@gmail.com with the dates and description of the disruption. Credits are the sole remedy for uptime failures and are not redeemable for cash.

5. Planned Maintenance

We will provide at least 48 hours advance notice of planned maintenance via email to account holders and via a notice on the status page. Planned maintenance is scheduled outside of 09:00–18:00 IST on weekdays where possible.

6. Data Availability

Because OpsManifest stores build data locally in your browser (IndexedDB), your data remains accessible even during a service outage — you can continue working offline. Cloud sync (Pro+) is additionally backed by Google Firebase with its own 99.95% SLA.

7. Reporting an Incident

To report a service issue, email sriram.c76@gmail.com with the subject line starting with the severity: [P1], [P2], [P3], or [P4], followed by a brief description.
