This Master Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Flourishing, the provider of OpsManifest ("Provider"), and the customer organisation named in the accompanying Order Form ("Customer").

1. Services

The Provider agrees to provide the Customer access to OpsManifest, an infrastructure lifecycle management tool, as described in the Order Form and subject to the terms of this Agreement. The specific plan, number of users, and pricing are set out in the Order Form.

The Provider will deliver the services substantially as described in the product documentation available at opsmanifest.pages.dev. The Provider reserves the right to update, improve, or modify features with reasonable notice to the Customer.

2. Term and Renewal

  • This Agreement begins on the date the Order Form is signed and continues for the initial term specified therein (typically 12 months).
  • Unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 30 days before the end of the term, the Agreement renews automatically for successive 12-month periods at the then-current pricing.
  • Either party may terminate this Agreement with 30 days written notice if the other party materially breaches the Agreement and fails to cure the breach within that notice period.

3. Fees and Payment

  • Fees are as agreed in the Order Form. The Provider will give 30 days written notice before increasing fees for existing customers.
  • For enterprise arrangements, payment is due within 30 days of invoice. Preferred payment methods: bank transfer, UPI (India), or as agreed.
  • Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month on the outstanding balance.
  • The Customer is responsible for all applicable taxes, duties, and levies on the fees.

4. Intellectual Property

  • Provider IP: OpsManifest, its source code, design, algorithms, and documentation are and remain the sole property of the Provider. This Agreement grants the Customer a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use the service during the term.
  • Customer data: All data entered by the Customer into OpsManifest (project information, design fields, build configurations, risk logs, etc.) remains the Customer's property at all times. The Provider claims no ownership over Customer data.
  • Outputs: Documents, spreadsheets, and exports generated by OpsManifest from Customer data may be used by the Customer without restriction — in CAB submissions, audit reports, client documentation, or any other business purpose.

5. Confidentiality

Each party agrees to keep the other's confidential information (including pricing, technical details, and Customer build data) strictly confidential, and not to disclose it to third parties without prior written consent, except as required by law. This obligation survives termination of the Agreement for 3 years.

The Provider will not disclose the Customer's identity or use the Customer's name in marketing without prior written consent.

6. Data Protection

The handling of personal data is governed by the Data Processing Agreement, which forms part of this Agreement. In summary:

  • Build data is stored locally in the Customer's browser by default (no cloud dependency for core use).
  • Cloud sync (if enabled) stores data in Google Firebase, scoped to the Customer's email, with access restricted to authenticated users.
  • The Provider does not sell or share Customer data with third parties for commercial purposes.

7. Service Levels

Service availability, support response times, and incident handling are governed by the Service Level Agreement, which forms part of this Agreement.

8. Limitation of Liability

  • OpsManifest is a planning and documentation tool. The Provider is not liable for production incidents, outages, data loss, financial loss, or regulatory penalties arising from decisions made using OpsManifest outputs.
  • The Provider's total aggregate liability under this Agreement in any 12-month period shall not exceed the total fees paid by the Customer in that period.
  • Neither party shall be liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages, loss of profits, or loss of business opportunity, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

9. Indemnification

The Customer agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the Provider from any claims, damages, or costs (including legal fees) arising from: (a) the Customer's misuse of OpsManifest; (b) the Customer's violation of applicable law; or (c) data the Customer enters into OpsManifest that infringes third-party rights.

10. Warranties

  • The Provider warrants that OpsManifest will perform substantially as described in the product documentation during the term.
  • The Provider does not warrant that the service will be uninterrupted, error-free, or suitable for any specific regulatory or compliance purpose.
  • The Customer warrants that it has the authority to enter this Agreement and that its use of the service will comply with applicable law.

11. Force Majeure

Neither party is liable for delays or failures caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, internet infrastructure failures, acts of government, or third-party service outages (including Firebase, Cloudflare, or Groq).

12. Governing Law and Disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. The parties agree to attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith negotiation before initiating formal proceedings. If negotiation fails, disputes shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, with proceedings conducted in English.

13. Entire Agreement

This Agreement, together with the Order Form, SLA, DPA, and AUP, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding the subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions and agreements.
