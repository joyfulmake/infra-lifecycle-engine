Acceptable Use Policy

Effective date: 21 June 2026

OpsManifest is built for professional infrastructure teams. This policy is short and practical — it describes what you can do freely, what is not permitted, and what happens if the policy is violated.

1. What You Can Do

  • Use OpsManifest to plan, document, and manage infrastructure migration and provisioning projects for your organisation or your clients.
  • Export build data (Gantt schedules, RTM documents, RAID logs, CAB packs) and use those exports in any professional context — internal documentation, client deliverables, regulatory submissions, CAB board presentations.
  • Share builds with team members within your organisation using the Team Workspace feature.
  • Use guest access to evaluate the tool before creating an account.
  • Run an offline (PWA or local build) copy of OpsManifest for testing within a secure enterprise environment.

2. What Is Not Permitted

  • Reselling or sublicensing: You may not resell, white-label, or provide access to OpsManifest as a standalone service to third parties without a written reseller agreement.
  • Reverse engineering: You may not decompile, disassemble, or attempt to extract the source code or algorithms of OpsManifest.
  • Automated abuse: You may not use automated scripts, bots, or crawlers to scrape, stress-test, or abuse the service or its APIs.
  • Security attacks: You may not attempt to gain unauthorised access to the service, its infrastructure, or other users' data.
  • Harmful content: You may not enter data designed to harm, harass, or defame individuals or organisations.
  • Circumventing plan limits: You may not create multiple accounts or use technical means to exceed the limits of your plan.
  • Illegal use: You may not use OpsManifest in connection with any activity that violates applicable law.
  • Credential sharing: Account credentials must not be shared between individuals. Team plans include per-user seats; sharing a single account across multiple users is a violation of the plan terms.

3. AI Feature Use

  • OpsMentor AI outputs (risk analysis, design suggestions, task enrichment) are generated by a large language model and may contain inaccuracies. You are responsible for verifying all AI outputs before using them in production decisions.
  • Do not enter personally sensitive data (employee personal details, patient data, classified or secret government information) into AI features. AI input is transmitted to a third-party API (Groq) for processing.
  • AI features are for professional planning use only — not for generating content intended to mislead, deceive, or defraud.

4. Data You Enter

  • You are responsible for ensuring that the data you enter into OpsManifest complies with your organisation's data handling policies and applicable law.
  • Do not enter classified, regulated, or highly sensitive personal data (HIPAA, PCI-DSS card data, biometric data) unless you have confirmed with us in writing that your deployment configuration meets the required standards.
  • OpsManifest is a planning tool for infrastructure teams. It is not certified as a system of record for compliance purposes (e.g. PCI-DSS QSA, HIPAA Business Associate). Consult your compliance team before using it in a regulated environment.

5. Enforcement

Violation of this policy may result in:

  • Temporary suspension of access while the issue is investigated
  • Permanent termination of the account without refund for serious or repeated violations
  • Legal action where required by law or to protect the rights of other users

We will make reasonable attempts to contact you before taking action, except where the violation poses an immediate risk to the service or other users.

6. Reporting Violations

If you believe another user is violating this policy, or if you have discovered a security vulnerability, please contact us at sriram.c76@gmail.com. Security disclosures are handled confidentially.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated to account holders by email with at least 14 days notice.
